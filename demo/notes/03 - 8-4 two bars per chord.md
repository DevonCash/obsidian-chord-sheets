Notating in 8/4 is a way to give each chord two 4/4 bars' worth of time.
At 120 BPM a bar is 8 quarter notes = 4 seconds.

Compare this against [[03b - 4-4 twin]], which is the same music written in 4/4
with each chord doubled. Run them side by side: every chord change should fall
at the same moment (0s, 4s, 8s, 12s) and the scroll should track identically.

The click is where they differ, and that difference is the point of the wider
meter: 8/4 accents every *other* downbeat, because `X___x___` spans what 4/4
has to write as two separate bars. The 4/4 twin can only accent every bar.

```chords
[Verse]
| Em | Am |
each chord gets two bars of four
| C  | G  |
and the scroll should crawl accordingly
```
