The 4/4 equivalent of [[03 - 8-4 two bars per chord]] — same tempo, each chord
written out twice instead of relying on the wider meter.

Chord changes and scrolling match the 8/4 version exactly. The click does not:
here every downbeat is accented, where 8/4 accents every other one.
