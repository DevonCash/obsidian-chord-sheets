A bar of repeat markers before the first chord counts you in: it holds its own
measure, so the metronome clicks through it and the scroll waits there while
nothing is highlighted. The first chord starts one bar late.

Bar lines are what make it a measure — a bare `%%%%` with no bar lines around it
counts for nothing, because a line without bar lines is measured by its chords.

One bar of count-in:

```chords
| % |
| Em      Am | C       G |
I heard there was a secret chord
```

Two bars:

```chords
| % | % |
| Em      Am | C       G |
that David played and it pleased the Lord
```

The same trick works mid-song wherever you want a bar of nothing.
